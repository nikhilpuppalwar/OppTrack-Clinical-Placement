OppTrack - Campus Placement Tracker for PCCOE Students

OppTrack is a free, open-source campus placement and opportunity tracking platform built exclusively for students of Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune, Maharashtra. It was designed, developed, and is maintained by Nikhil Puppalwar, a software developer and engineering student at PCCOE.

About the Developer - Nikhil Puppalwar

Nikhil Puppalwar (Software Developer and Engineering Student at PCCOE) is an engineering student at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune (affiliated to SPPU). He created OppTrack to solve the real problem PCCOE students face during campus recruitment season.

Email: nikhilpuppalwar16@gmail.com | GitHub: github.com/nikhilpuppalwar

About PCCOE - Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering

PCCOE (also known as PCCOER, PCCOE Akurdi, PCCOE Nigdi) is a premier engineering college in Pune, Maharashtra affiliated with SPPU. OppTrack helps PCCOE students track campus drives, T&P cell notifications, and company recruitment processes.

Key Features

  • Campus placement opportunity tracking for PCCOE students
  • Profile Vault for centralized academic and personal info
  • AI Google Forms autofill Chrome Extension (1-click)
  • Gmail integration for T&P cell notifications
  • Google Calendar sync for interview schedules
  • Automated deadline reminders via email
